# TurnsIntoSquishy
Turn two pictures into a soft, squishy 3D plush you can poke, stretch, squeeze and spin, right in your browser.  Upload a front and a back picture, and the site inflates the shape into a puffy pillow-like model. Until then, you can play with a squishy ball.

# Features
- Pictures to 3D: Upload a front and a back image (PNG or JPEG). The outline is detected automatically, inflated into a plush shape, and the pictures are wrapped onto the front and back.

- Squishy physics: Drag the object to pull and push the surface. It wobbles like jelly when you let go.

- Squeeze: Double-tap and hold to squash the whole object, then release for a bouncy wobble.

- Free rotation: Drag empty space to spin the object in any direction, with smooth gliding after you let go.

- Zoom: Mouse wheel, trackpad pinch or two-finger pinch. The camera stays focused on the object.

- Works on phones and desktop: Touch and mouse are both supported.

- Private: Pictures are processed in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

# Built with
- three.js for 3D rendering.
  
- Vanilla JavaScript, with no framework.
  
- Including claude for AI assistance.
